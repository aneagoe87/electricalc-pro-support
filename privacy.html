Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 August 2026

In short

Electrical Pro Toolkit collects no personal data. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking of any kind. Your calculations never leave your device. The only information ever sent off the device is a pair of coordinates, only when you personally tap Calculate Yield in the Solar Estimator.

What is stored on your device

One thing: your chosen language, so the app opens in it next time. It stays on your device and is removed when you delete the app.

Calculator inputs and results are held in memory while a screen is open and are not written to storage or transmitted anywhere.

Location

The Solar Estimator has a Use Current GPS Location button. If you tap it, the app asks for permission to read your location while the app is in use, and fills the latitude and longitude fields with the result.

  • Location is read only when you tap that button. Never in the background.
  • It is used only to populate those two fields, which you can also type by hand.
  • It is not stored, logged, or associated with you in any way.
  • Declining the permission leaves every part of the app usable — enter coordinates manually.

The one network request

When you tap Calculate Yield in the Solar Estimator, the app sends the latitude, longitude, peak power and system loss values shown on screen to PVGIS, the Photovoltaic Geographical Information System operated by the European Commission's Joint Research Centre, and displays the estimate it returns.

That request carries no identifier, no device information and nothing about you — only those four numbers. It is the only outbound request the app makes. PVGIS handles it under the European Commission's own privacy terms: ec.europa.eu/info/privacy-policy_en.

If you never open the Solar Estimator, the app makes no network requests at all.

What the app does not do

Accounts or sign-inNone. The app has no user accounts.
AnalyticsNone. No usage or crash analytics SDK is present.
AdvertisingNone. No ad networks, no ad identifier.
TrackingNone, as App Tracking Transparency defines it. Nothing is shared with data brokers or used to build a profile.
Contacts, photos, camera, microphoneNever requested.
Selling dataThere is no data to sell.

Children

The app is a professional tool and is not directed at children. Since it collects no personal data from anyone, it collects none from children either.

Your rights

Under the GDPR and similar laws you have rights to access, correct and erase personal data held about you. No personal data about you is collected or held, so there is nothing to retrieve or erase on request. Deleting the app removes the single stored language preference.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here with a new date. A change that materially affects how data is handled will also be described in the app's release notes.
